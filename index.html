How it works

A simple way to create and share a lasting memorial

Build a digital memorial, personalize it with photographs and tributes, then share it with family and friends.

01

Create an account

Sign up as an individual or use a funeral parlour account to manage memorials for families.

02

Create the memorial

Add the person's name, dates, tribute, biography, photographs, audio and other meaningful memories.

03

Personalize it

Choose a memorial template, colours and fonts, add a gallery, background and emblem, and preview the result.

04

Share & remember

Publish the memorial, generate a QR code and share the memorial link with loved ones.
